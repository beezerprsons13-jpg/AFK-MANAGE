Our Story

AFK Management began with a simple goal: provide dependable service that property owners could trust without constant follow-up. The business was built around hard work, attention to detail, and the belief that good service is personal service.

What started as local property care work expanded into a broader service model built for businesses, homeowners, and public-facing spaces that need regular upkeep and consistent standards.

Local roots

AFK Management was founded to serve the Wilkesboro, NC community with direct, hands-on work and practical solutions.

Growing service

As more homes, offices, and facilities needed reliable care, the business expanded to include both custodial and landscaping support.

Today

Today, AFK Management continues to focus on quality workmanship, responsive service, and the long-term care of the properties we maintain.
